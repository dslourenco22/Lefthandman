Senior Cybersecurity Engineer
Omni Control Technology — Information Security Team

About the Role
Omni Control Technology is seeking a Senior Cybersecurity Engineer to strengthen
the security posture of our industrial control systems and corporate infrastructure.
You will lead threat detection, incident response, and security architecture efforts
across cloud and on-premise environments.

Responsibilities
- Design and maintain SIEM detection rules and monitoring dashboards
- Lead incident response investigations and forensic analysis
- Perform vulnerability assessments and coordinate remediation
- Harden cloud infrastructure on Microsoft Azure and AWS
- Mentor junior analysts and collaborate with DevOps on secure pipelines

Required Skills
- 5+ years of experience in cybersecurity or information security
- Strong hands-on experience with SIEM platforms (Splunk, QRadar, or similar)
- Network security, firewalls, and intrusion detection systems
- Incident response and threat hunting
- Scripting with Python for security automation
- Familiarity with cloud security on Azure and AWS

Preferred Skills
- Experience with Kubernetes and container security
- Knowledge of SOAR platforms
- Threat intelligence and malware analysis
- Experience securing industrial control systems (ICS/SCADA)

Certifications
- CISSP or CompTIA Security+ required
- CEH or OSCP preferred

Education
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Security, or related field

Soft Skills
- Strong communication and documentation skills
- Leadership and mentoring ability
- Analytical and detail-oriented
